02 / Film

On the big screen.

Ik ga voor je bidden

I play the lead role of Revo in Ik ga voor je bidden, a Dutch short film about a silent father-son bond where the love is there, but the words are not.

The film was directed and written by Jalu Glissenaar, produced by Jefferson van den Broek and made at the HKU. It was selected for the NFF Studentencompetitie 2026.

On 26 September 2026 it was shown to the public at Kinepolis Jaarbeurs in Utrecht, during the Nederlands Film Festival.
